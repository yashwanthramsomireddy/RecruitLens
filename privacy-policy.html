RecruitLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RecruitLens ("we", "our", "the app") is built and operated by TeamExyKings. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

1. What We Collect

  • Account info: name, email, phone number, company/domain (if applicable)
  • Resume data: when you upload a resume, we extract the text content and analyze it with AI. We do not store the original file — only the extracted text and the analysis results (score, matched/missing skills, summary, etc.)
  • Usage data: which AI models you use, scan counts, credit balance — used to enforce plan limits and for your own usage history
  • Payment data: processed entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card/UPI details — only the transaction outcome (success/failure, amount, credits granted)

2. How We Use It

  • To provide the resume screening service itself
  • To enforce plan limits (Free/Pro scan caps, credit balances)
  • To send transactional emails (interview scheduling, invites, approvals) — never marketing spam
  • To improve the app based on aggregate usage patterns (never by reading individual resumes for any purpose other than the analysis you requested)

3. Third Parties We Use

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • OpenRouter — routes your resume text to the AI model you select for analysis
  • Brevo — sends transactional emails on our behalf
  • Razorpay — payment processing

None of these third parties use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to us.

4. Data Retention

Candidate analysis data is kept as long as your account is active. You can delete individual candidates at any time from within the app. Account deletion requests can be sent to the contact below.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, or request deletion of your account and associated data, by contacting us below.
